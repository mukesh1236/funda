# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as a statement of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by `NNNN-title.md` (link it once it exists)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Implemented in:** commit(s), or "not yet"

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the incident, the measurement. Facts, not
opinions; include the numbers if there are any.

## Decision

What we chose, in one or two sentences, stated actively ("We use X").

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we are now committed to. Be
honest about the costs; a record with only upsides is not a record.

## Alternatives considered

Each option and the specific reason it lost.
