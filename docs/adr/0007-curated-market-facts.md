# 7. Market facts are curated, never LLM-generated

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-05
- **Implemented in:** `a88a3c5`; `web/app.js` (`FACTS`)

## Context

Several slow paths are network-bound and cannot be optimised away (SEC fetches, LLM
calls). Showing a short market fact during the wait turns dead time into learning.
The facts could be generated on demand or written by hand.

## Decision

Facts are **hand-written, contextual and verifiable**: structural, regulatory and
definitional truths about funds and filings. They are never generated by an LLM,
never performance statistics, and never advice. A fact appears only after a wait has
already passed 600 ms, so it never delays a result.

## Consequences

- No hallucination risk on a finance product. A wrong "fun fact" is the same failure
  as a fabricated number, only friendlier.
- No latency added to the thing the fact exists to mask, and no spend against the AI
  budget.
- A small, finite pool that someone has to maintain and re-check for truth.
- Users can switch them off (per browser, `localStorage`).

## Alternatives considered

- **LLM-generated facts:** unbounded variety, but hallucination risk, added latency,
  and AI budget spent on decoration.
- **Live statistics from the app's own data:** verifiable, but needs a new public
  endpoint, and the existing admin stats carry private data (user counts, signups,
  per-user pins) that must never be exposed. Possible later as a separate,
  aggregate-only endpoint.
