# Market facts during waits

Several paths here are network-bound and cannot be optimised away: SEC EDGAR
fetches, N-PORT downloads, yfinance batches, LLM calls. While one is running, the
loading state shows a short, true, relevant fact about funds and filings, so dead
time reads as a moment of learning rather than a spinner.

## How it works

One seam in `web/app.js`:

- **`waitingHtml(message, topic)`** returns the loading block plus a hidden fact
  slot. Eleven call sites use it: stock detail, analysts, fact sheet
  (open and poll), fund drivers (including the 30-second first run), fund detail,
  compare, and X-Ray (first load and poll).
- **One app-wide ticker** (400 ms) reveals and rotates facts. It starts on demand
  and stops itself as soon as no slot is left on the page.
- Callers manage **no lifecycle**. Every call site already replaces `innerHTML`
  when its data arrives, which removes the slot and ends its rotation. Nothing to
  tear down, nothing to leak.

## Rules that must not change

1. **A fact never delays a result.** It appears only after a wait has already
   passed `_FACT_DELAY_MS` (600 ms). Anything quicker renders with no fact at all,
   because a fact that flashes up and vanishes is worse than none. Nothing is ever
   held back so the user can "finish reading".
2. **Facts are hand-written and verifiable.** Structural, regulatory and
   definitional truths: what Form 497K is, what a basis point is, why an expense
   ratio never appears on a statement. **No performance statistics, no unsourced
   claims, and nothing generated by an LLM.** A wrong fun fact on a financial
   product is the same failure as a fabricated number, and generating one would add
   latency to the thing it exists to mask.
3. **Contextual.** The pool matches the wait: filing facts on a fact-sheet wait,
   N-PORT and overlap facts on X-Ray and holdings waits. Pools in `FACTS`:
   `factsheet`, `holdings`, `xray`, `drivers`, `compare`, `stock`, `analysts`, and
   a `default`.
4. **User control.** Facts are on by default. A **Hide** link on the fact switches
   them off; a **Market facts** switch in the account menu (the avatar in the header)
   switches them back on. The menu is reachable on mobile too, which is why it is not
   a desktop-only control.
5. **Failure must not matter.** The preference is `localStorage['facts_enabled']`,
   defaulting to on. Every access is wrapped in try/catch because `localStorage`
   throws in private windows and when site data is blocked.

## Accessibility

The message is the live region and is announced once. The rotating fact is
`aria-hidden`: announcing every rotation would spam screen readers that already
heard the status. The Hide button sits outside the `aria-hidden` element so it
stays reachable. The fade-in is wrapped in `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Deliberately not covered

Error states, the admin telemetry loaders, the WhatsApp link-code loader, and the
fact-sheet *answer* slot keep the plain spinner. A rotating fact next to a pending
answer would read as the answer itself.

## Adding a fact

Add a string to the right pool in `FACTS`. Before you do, check it is **true
without a date attached**, **not a statistic**, and **not advice**. If you cannot
cite a regulation or a definition for it, do not add it.
